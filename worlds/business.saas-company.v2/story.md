# Northstar Relay, late August

Northstar Relay is a ten-person SaaS company that moves large operational exports
between business systems. The product is good, the company is healthy, and the
team is stretched in ordinary ways.

Lumen Labs is the largest customer up for renewal this quarter. Its interactive
exports work, but scheduled exports above about 50,000 rows stop at the two-minute
limit. Priya Raman at Lumen has been patient. She now needs an answer before her
renewal meeting. The team has reproduced the fault and narrowed it to the worker,
but the fix is not merged.

Lumen also has invoice 4471 for 412.00 USD open until August 30. The invoice is not
late and it is not the cause of the renewal risk. It is still part of the account
picture, so the person preparing the renewal brief must not claim that Lumen has
paid it.

Release 2.8 is due next week. It includes an export-worker change and a new audit
page. Pulling the timeout fix into the release is possible, but it adds risk. The
release thread contains a real disagreement: Lucas wants to merge the focused fix;
Jon wants another load test first.

Theo Martin starts on Monday. Imani has prepared the onboarding page, but the
staging access request is still open. This is not a crisis. It is the kind of small
loose end that makes the company feel occupied rather than staged.

Around these stories are normal invoices, vendor bills, newsletters, calendar
changes, code reviews, and low-priority customer mail. The background data exists
to make the workspace believable. It must not drown the four active stories.
